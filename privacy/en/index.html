Privacy policy

“Salva il tuo budget” app for Android · Last updated: 15 November 2026

In short
  • Your data (accounts, transactions, categories, budgets, reminders) stays only on your phone.
  • There is no account, no server and no advertising.
  • The app does not collect usage statistics and does not share data with anyone.

1. Who we are

“Salva il tuo budget” is an app to keep track of personal income, expenses and budgets, made by an independent developer. For any privacy question you can write to salvailtuobudget@gmail.com.

2. What data the app uses

The app only stores the data you enter: account names and balances, transactions with amount, date, category and notes, recurring transactions, budgets, reminders and your settings (for example the theme). This data is saved in a database on your device and is not sent to us or to third parties.

3. PIN and fingerprint

If you set a PIN, only an encrypted version of it (a “hash”) is saved on the phone, in Android’s secure storage: the plain PIN is never stored.

If you enable fingerprint unlock, recognition is handled entirely by Android: the app only receives the result (“recognised” or “not recognised”) and never has access to your fingerprint. This is why the app asks for the “Use biometric data” permission.

4. Backup and export

From the settings you can export a backup file or a CSV file of your transactions. The file is created on the phone and you choose where to share it (for example Google Drive or an email): from then on it is handled by the service you chose, under its own privacy policy. The backup does not contain the PIN.

If Android’s automatic backup is enabled on your phone, Google may save the app’s database in your Google account so it can be restored on a new phone. This is an Android feature, managed by Google, that you can turn off in the phone settings. The PIN is excluded from this backup.

5. When you write to us

With “Invia un suggerimento” (Send feedback) the app opens your email app with a message addressed to us: it is only sent if you send it. At the bottom of the text you will find the app and Android versions, which help us understand any problem; you can delete them before sending. Your email address and the content of the message are used only to reply to you, are not shared with anyone, and you can ask for them to be deleted at any time.

6. What we do NOT do

  • We do not use advertising, tracking or analytics tools.
  • We do not sell or share data.
  • The app is not connected to your bank and does not access other data on the phone (contacts, location, photos…).

7. Your rights and how to delete your data

Since the data stays on your phone, you are in control: you can edit or delete it in the app, and uninstalling the app (or clearing its data from the Android settings) removes it completely. For emails you sent us, you can request access or deletion by writing to salvailtuobudget@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect data about them.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. Important changes will also be mentioned in the app’s release notes.
